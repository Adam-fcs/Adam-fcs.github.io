Goodbye to Edmonton

Edmonton, Canada

After seeing so many friends say goodbye to Edmonton over the years, it's finally my turn. This chapter of studying abroad has become one of the most meaningful parts of my life. It has shaped who I am today and given me the ability to choose the path I truly want to follow. Thank you to everyone who made my life in Canada so rich, memorable, and full of joy. I'll always cherish these wonderful years and every person I had the privilege of meeting along the way. My story in Canada has come to an end. I will miss my life here and hopefully come back for a visit. I can't wait to go back to my home island -- Hainan, meet my family, speak the local dialect, and taste all the delicious food!
